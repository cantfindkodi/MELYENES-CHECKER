The toolkit.

Five tools. One account. No tracking. Just paste and go.

01 CCN / CVV

Card Checker

Validates card numbers with the Luhn algorithm. Detects Visa, MC, Amex, Discover, JCB, UnionPay.

02 BIN

BIN Lookup

Identifies issuer, card type, brand, and country from the first 6–8 digits of a card number.

03 3DS

OTP / 3DS Check

Checks 3-D Secure enrollment status — VBV for Visa, MBV for MasterCard, or NVBV if not enrolled.

04 ADYEN

Adyen Encrypt

Client-side card encryption compatible with Adyen's payment gateway. AES-256-CBC.

05 CLOVER

Clover Token

Generates Clover POS-compatible encrypted card tokens for payment processing.

Why melyenes?

Most "checker" sites are sketchy ad farms that phone home with your data. We got tired of it, so we built something better.

  • Nothing leaves your machine. Every check, every encryption runs 100% in your browser's JS engine.
  • Secure access only. Sign in once, use everything. We keep freeloaders out so the tools stay fast.
  • Open logic, no black boxes. Luhn, BIN detection — standard algorithms you can verify yourself.
  • Fast, because there's no server. Sub-millisecond responses. No spinners. No "please wait."
